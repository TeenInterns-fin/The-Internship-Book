Dear School Leaders and Counselors,

Thank you.

For being the silent architects behind every young life that walks through your corridors. For caring not just about grades, but about growth. Not just about discipline, but about direction.

The Internship Book is more than a project — it's a movement. To help teens build career clarity, character, and courage before the world demands it of them. And your role in this movement is foundational.

As educators, you've seen the shift. Today's teens are not just digital natives — they're deeply intuitive, restless for purpose, overwhelmed by choice, and hungry for relevance. They're not just asking "what do I want to become?" They're asking "why does it matter?"

Internships, when done right, are not a checkbox. They are a mirror and a map. They allow students to see their strengths in action, fail safely, ask questions, shadow professionals, and discover who they are — outside the classroom.

This section is for you!

This book belongs in your toolkit. Not just to encourage internships, but to reimagine how we introduce teens to the world of work — in a way that's age-appropriate, bite-sized, experiential, and real.

In your hands, this book becomes more than a resource. It becomes a bridge. Between what's taught and what's lived. Between who they are and who they're becoming.

You've always shaped futures. But now, you have a chance to help shape calling — not just careers.

Let's walk with our teens. Not just to prepare them for exams, but to prepare them for life. Let's show them that school is not just the end of childhood — but the beginning of meaningful impact.

Thank you for partnering with us.

Together, we're not just educating students. We're equipping leaders.

Core Resources to Build an Internship Department

Internship Program Framework

  • Clear goals (exploration, skilling, exposure, or career clarity)
  • Types of internships: Micro, Mega, Volunteering, Shadowing
  • Age-appropriate pathways (Grade 8–12)
  • Skill categories (aligned with NEP & WEF future skills)

Internship Handbook

  • Guide for students, parents, and companies
  • Includes timelines, expectations, etiquette, and FAQs
  • Printable + digital versions

Student Portfolio Templates

  • Resume/CV template for high schoolers
  • Reflective journal/logbook
  • Project submission format
  • Feedback & recommendation request format

Partnership & Outreach Kit

  • Pitch deck and email templates to reach companies, startups, NGOs
  • Sample MoUs
  • Explainer on how school internships benefit organizations
  • Framework to track companies per industry cluster

Parent Communication Toolkit

  • Consent letter formats
  • Internship info deck for orientation
  • WhatsApp & email updates templates
  • FAQs for hesitant parents

Assessment & Matching Tools

  • Internship Readiness Assessment (like TeenInterns roadmap)
  • Student interest & strength mapping
  • Matching logic or counselor dashboard
  • Pre & post-internship self-evaluation forms

Mentor & Host Onboarding Kit

  • Onboarding guide for external mentors
  • Session structure for learning days
  • Feedback formats for hosts
  • Honor certificate for companies

Digital Tracking Dashboard

  • To track who's interning, where, and when
  • Hours logged, feedback, certifications
  • Career themes emerging in each student

Training & Orientation Modules

  • For students: professionalism, communication, LinkedIn, etc.
  • For teachers/counselors: how to run reflection sessions
  • For mentors: how to engage high schoolers

Reflection & Showcase Templates

  • Presentation Day or Internship Showcase Day structure
  • Templates for student presentations
  • Certificates and recognition formats
  • Reflection prompts for journals

Internship Board or Directory

  • Curated list of verified partners (NGOs, startups, professionals)
  • Categorized by industry, skill, format (offline/online)
  • Updated quarterly

Internship Club / ILM Circle for Teens

  • Peer-led discussion groups on career themes
  • Invite alumni/interns to speak
  • Monthly "Internship Sparks" events

Feedback & Improvement Loop

  • Exit surveys from students, hosts, parents
  • Review meetings once a term
  • Data-driven improvements to the program
